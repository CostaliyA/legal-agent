# H-1B Employer Filing Checklist — January 17, 2025

**Scope:** Employer-side filing checklist for H-1B petitions filed on or after January 17, 2025, using only the supplied excerpts. This checklist covers the requested topics: specialty occupation, owner beneficiaries, work availability, amendments, cap-gap treatment, evidence, and filing dates. It is not a complete H-1B checklist; the excerpted specialty-occupation documentary list at 8 CFR 214.2(h)(4)(iv) is partially truncated, so the full rule and form instructions must be consulted.

## 1. Authority and effective-date gate

- [ ] **Confirm the petition is filed on or after January 17, 2025.** The modernization final rule is effective January 17, 2025, and applies to petitions filed on or after that date. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 1; p. 17; p. 105.`
- [ ] **Apply the final rule’s standards to both initial and extension petitions filed on or after January 17, 2025.** DHS states the changes apply to “any H–1B petition filed on or after this date, whether it is a petition seeking an initial grant of H–1B status or extension of H–1B status.” `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 17.`
- [ ] **Do not treat the October 23, 2023 NPRM as operative law except where finalized.** The final rule “adopts many of the provisions proposed in the NPRM, with revisions.” `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, pp. 2–3; p. 5.`
- [ ] **Separate the registration final rule from the modernization final rule.** The registration rule was effective March 4, 2024, and established beneficiary-centric selection, integrity measures, and start-date flexibility; DHS later states it went into effect for the FY 2025 cap season. `h1b-registration-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 1; h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 5; p. 105.`
- [ ] **Do not checklist the related-entity multiple-registration proposal as final.** DHS expressly states it “will not finalize” the proposed change at 8 CFR 214.2(h)(2)(i)(G) that would expressly state related entities are prohibited from submitting multiple H-1B registrations for the same individual. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 3.`
- [ ] **Do not treat public comments or advocacy as requirements.** Comments urging Congress to act or USCIS to lobby are not operative filing requirements. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 105.`

## 2. Specialty occupation

- [ ] **Draft the position to require at least a bachelor’s degree or higher in a directly related specific specialty, or its equivalent, as the minimum for entry into the occupation in the United States.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **Do not rely on a general degree without further specialization.** “A position is not a specialty occupation if attainment of a general degree, without further specialization, is sufficient to qualify for the position.” `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **If the position allows a range of degree fields, confirm each field is directly related to the duties.** “Directly related” means there is a logical connection between the required degree, or its equivalent, and the duties of the position. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **Establish at least one of the four specialty-occupation criteria:**
  1. A U.S. baccalaureate or higher degree in a directly related specific specialty, or equivalent, is normally the minimum requirement for entry into the occupation;
  2. Such a degree is normally required for parallel positions among similar organizations in the employer’s industry in the United States;
  3. The employer — or the third party if the beneficiary will be staffed to that third party — normally requires such a degree to perform the job duties; or
  4. The specific duties are so specialized, complex, or unique that the knowledge required is normally associated with such a degree.  
  `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **Apply “normally” correctly.** For these criteria, “normally” means conforming to a type, standard, or regular pattern, and is characterized by what is usual, typical, common, or routine. “Normally does not mean always.” `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **If the beneficiary will be staffed to a third party, confirm the actual work is in a specialty occupation and that the third party’s requirements control.** If the beneficiary is contracted to fill a position in the third party’s organization and becomes part of that hierarchy, it is the third party’s requirements — not the petitioner’s — that are most relevant. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, pp. 144–145; p. 3.`
- [ ] **Do not treat the certified LCA as a specialty-occupation determination.** DOL certification of an LCA in an occupational classification does not constitute a determination that the occupation is a specialty occupation; USCIS determines whether the LCA involves a specialty occupation and corresponds with the petition, and whether all other eligibility requirements are met. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 144.`
- [ ] **Confirm the position is bona fide and available as of the requested start date.** At filing, the petitioner must establish it has a bona fide position in a specialty occupation available for the beneficiary as of the start date of the requested validity period; it need not establish specific day-to-day assignments for the entire requested period. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`

**Note:** DHS characterizes the specialty-occupation changes as codifying existing practices and states they are not intended or expected to make current H-1B nonimmigrants ineligible based on employment already found to be a specialty occupation; DHS also states the rule does not narrow or change the existing standards for qualifying through a combination of education, training, or experience. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 17.`

## 3. Owner beneficiaries

- [ ] **Determine whether the beneficiary has a controlling interest in the petitioner.** A controlling interest means the beneficiary owns more than 50 percent of the petitioner or has majority voting rights in the petitioner. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 4; pp. 145–146.`
- [ ] **If the beneficiary has a controlling interest, the petition must show that any duties directly related to owning and directing the business are consistent with specialty-occupation duties.** Such a beneficiary may perform duties directly related to owning and directing the petitioner’s business only as long as the beneficiary will perform specialty-occupation duties a majority of the time, consistent with the terms of the H-1B petition. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **Request the correct validity period.** Initial approval of a petition filed by a U.S. employer in which the beneficiary has a controlling interest is limited to a validity period of up to 18 months. The first extension — including an amended petition with a request for an extension of stay — is also limited to up to 18 months. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 146.`

## 4. Work availability and U.S. employer requirements

- [ ] **Confirm the petitioner qualifies as a “United States employer.”** The definition requires: (1) a bona fide job offer for the beneficiary to work within the United States, which may include telework, remote work, or other off-site work within the United States; (2) legal presence in the United States and amenability to service of process; and (3) an IRS Tax identification number. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **Document that the position is available as of the requested start date.** The petitioner must establish a bona fide specialty-occupation position available for the beneficiary as of the start date of the requested validity period. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **Do not over-document day-to-day assignments for the entire requested period.** The final rule states the petitioner is not required to establish specific day-to-day assignments for the entire time requested. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **If staffed to a third party, confirm the third party’s specialty-occupation requirements and be prepared to submit contracts, work orders, or similar evidence if requested.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, pp. 144–145.`

## 5. Amendments and material changes

- [ ] **File an amended or new petition for any material change in the terms and conditions of employment or the beneficiary’s eligibility as specified in the original approved petition.** The amended or new petition must be filed with the appropriate fee and in accordance with form instructions. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **Include a current or new Department of Labor determination with the amended or new H-1B petition.** For H-1B, this includes a current or new certified labor condition application. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **File before the material change takes place.** The beneficiary is not authorized to work under materially changed terms and conditions until the new or amended H-1B petition is approved and takes effect, unless the beneficiary is eligible for H-1B portability under 8 CFR 214.2(h)(2)(i)(H). `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **Treat a change in place of employment to a geographic area requiring a corresponding LCA to be certified to USCIS as a material change.** An amended or new petition must be filed before the H-1B worker begins work at the new place of employment. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **Check the no-amendment exceptions where there are no material changes:**
  - Moving the beneficiary to a new job location within the same area of intended employment listed on the certified LCA supporting the current H-1B approval. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
  - Short-term placement(s) or assignment(s) outside the area of intended employment, subject to the regulatory conditions. The excerpt begins “for a total of 30 da” and is truncated; verify the exact day count and related conditions in the full rule. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, pp. 143–144.`
  - Non-worksite location for employee development, little time at any one location, or a peripatetic job. For peripatetic work, the excerpt states thresholds of not exceeding 5 consecutive workdays for any one visit by a peripatetic worker, or 10 consecutive workdays for a worker who spends most work time at one location and travels occasionally to other locations. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 144.`
- [ ] **Calendar revocation risk.** The final rule lists, among revocation grounds, failure to timely file an amended petition notifying USCIS of a material change or otherwise failing to comply with material-change requirements. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 146.`

## 6. Cap-gap treatment

- [ ] **For an F-1 student beneficiary of a cap-subject H-1B petition requesting a change of status, confirm the automatic cap-gap extension conditions.** The final regulatory text provides that the F-1 student’s duration of status, and any employment authorization granted under 8 CFR 274a.12(c)(3)(i)(B) or (C), is automatically extended until April 1 of the fiscal year for which H-1B status is requested or the validity start date of the approved petition, whichever is earlier, if the petition:
  1. Was timely filed;
  2. Requests an H-1B employment start date in the fiscal year for which H-1B status is requested, consistent with 8 CFR 214.2(h)(2)(i)(I); and
  3. Is nonfrivolous.  
  `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **Resolve the cap-gap date conflict by document role and operative text.** The background excerpt at p. 5 describes prior cap-gap amendments extending to October 1. The operative final regulatory text excerpt at p. 143 states the automatic extension runs to April 1 of the requested fiscal year or the approved petition’s validity start date, whichever is earlier. For a January 17, 2025 filing, use the final regulatory text and verify the full rule for the specific case. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 5; p. 143.`
- [ ] **If the petition is not timely filed, is not cap-subject, does not request change of status, is frivolous, or does not request an H-1B start date in the relevant fiscal year, do not assume cap-gap under this text.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`

## 7. Evidence and documentation

- [ ] **File on Form I-129 with the fee prescribed by 8 CFR 106.2 and the initial evidence specified in 8 CFR 214.2 for an extension or amendment.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, pp. 142–143.`
- [ ] **Include the required LCA.** For an amended or new H-1B petition, include a current or new certified LCA. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **For extension or amendment requests, include evidence of maintenance of status.** Supporting evidence may include copies of paystubs, W-2 forms, quarterly wage reports, tax returns, contracts, and work orders. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **Prepare specialty-occupation evidence showing the degree requirement, duties, and at least one criterion.** The excerpted rule states an H-1B petition involving a specialty occupation must be accompanied by the items in 8 CFR 214.2(h)(4)(iv), but the excerpt truncates the full list; verify the complete list in the full rule. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **If there is a contractual relationship, be prepared for USCIS to request contracts, work orders, or similar evidence between all parties showing the bona fide nature of the beneficiary’s position and the minimum educational requirements to perform the duties.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **Include evidence that the position is bona fide and available as of the requested start date.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **If the beneficiary has a controlling interest, include petition terms showing the ownership/voting interest and that specialty-occupation duties will occupy a majority of the beneficiary’s time.** The excerpts do not prescribe a specific ownership-evidence list; confirm with the full rule and form instructions. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 145.`
- [ ] **Dependents:** Dependents holding derivative status may be included in the petition only if it is for one worker and the form version specifically provides for their inclusion; otherwise, dependents should file extensions of stay on Form I-539. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **Be prepared for verification and inspection.** USCIS may verify petition information through lawful means, including inspections and interviews; inability to verify facts due to failure or refusal to cooperate may result in denial or revocation. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 144.`
- [ ] **Review deference to prior approvals.** For an I-129 involving the same parties and same underlying facts, USCIS gives deference to its prior eligibility determination, but need not give deference if there was a material error, a material change in circumstances or eligibility requirements, or new material adverse information. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`

## 8. Filing dates and calendar items

- [ ] **Use January 17, 2025 as the modernization final-rule cutoff.** The rule is effective January 17, 2025, and applies to petitions filed on or after that date. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 1; p. 17; p. 105.`
- [ ] **For petitions filed before January 17, 2025, do not apply this final rule’s changes under the excerpted effective-date statements.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 17.`
- [ ] **Account for the separate registration rule effective March 4, 2024.** It implemented beneficiary-centric selection, start-date flexibility, and integrity measures and was in effect for the FY 2025 cap season. `h1b-registration-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 1; h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 5; p. 105.`
- [ ] **File any amended or new petition before the material change occurs.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **For controlling-interest beneficiaries, calendar up to 18 months for the initial approval and up to 18 months for the first extension, including an amended petition with an extension request.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 146.`
- [ ] **For cap-gap, calendar the April 1/validity-start-date rule from the operative text and verify the specific fiscal-year dates.** `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 143.`
- [ ] **Unresolved filing-date convention:** the excerpts do not state whether “filed” means postmark, receipt, or electronic submission for every filing method. Confirm in the form instructions and USCIS filing guidance. `h1b-modernization-final-rule-2024.pdf, p. 17; p. 105.`

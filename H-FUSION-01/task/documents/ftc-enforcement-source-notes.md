# Source notes: FTC telehealth privacy enforcement

**Use.** Newly prepared summaries of the linked official records for this task; the attached original BetterHelp complaint, final order, proposed consent package, and public-comment analysis are the primary matter documents. Allegations and pre-final proposals must be distinguished from the operative final order.

## GoodRx

The FTC describes GoodRx as its first enforcement action under the Health Breach Notification Rule, based on alleged disclosures of consumers' health information and failure to provide required breach notices. The agency's official case page links the complaint and final stipulated order. Treat the complaint as allegations and the final order as the binding settlement instrument.

Primary source: [FTC, GoodRx enforcement action](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/02/ftc-enforcement-action-bar-goodrx-sharing-consumers-sensitive-health-info-advertising); [FTC final stipulated order (PDF)](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/goodrxfinalstipulatedorder.pdf).

## BetterHelp

The FTC's final order prohibits specified disclosures of health information for advertising, requires affirmative express consent for specified disclosures, deletion requests to recipients, a privacy program, and retention limits. The final order—not the complaint, proposed consent, or public-comment explanation—controls the company's obligations. BetterHelp's complaint states allegations, not adjudicated findings.

Primary source: [FTC case timeline and finalization record](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/betterhelp-inc-matter-timeline-item-2023-07-14); the unedited final order, complaint, proposed consent, and public-comment analysis are attached in this task's `documents/` directory.

## Research limitation

The source notes are navigation aids. For a conclusion about a particular remedy, cite and verify the corresponding paragraph in the attached final order or the linked GoodRx order. Practitioner commentary is not used as a source of legal obligation.

# Source notes: FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (HBNR)

**Use and date.** These notes summarize the primary legal text for this task as of 1 August 2024. They are newly prepared research notes, not a copy of a Harvey LAB task document. The eCFR is an authoritative but unofficial online rendition; consult the linked Federal Register publication and statute for the formal source text.

## Operative provisions

- **Scope and covered entities — 16 CFR §§ 318.1–318.2.** The rule implements 42 U.S.C. § 17937. It covers vendors of personal health records, PHR-related entities, and third-party service providers that maintain information of U.S. citizens or residents, without importing the FTC Act's jurisdictional tests. It excludes HIPAA-covered entities and activities performed as a HIPAA business associate. The defined categories and the health-information/identifiability elements must be tested against the app's actual data flows.
- **Who must be notified — § 318.3.** A covered vendor or PHR-related entity notifies affected U.S. individuals and the FTC; media notice is required when 500 or more residents of one State or jurisdiction are affected. A third-party service provider reports to the covered vendor/entity.
- **Timing — § 318.4.** Individual and media notices, and service-provider notices, are due without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after discovery. FTC notice for incidents involving 500 or more people is contemporaneous with individual notice. Logged notices for incidents involving fewer than 500 people may be submitted annually within 60 calendar days after calendar year end.
- **Methods and content — §§ 318.5–318.6.** Individual notice is written (mail or qualifying email); urgent phone or other notice supplements the written notice. The rule also addresses substitute notice, media notice, and required notice contents.
- **Enforcement and dates — §§ 318.7–318.9.** Violations are treated as violations of an FTC Act rule and may attract civil penalties. The rule applies to breaches discovered on or after 24 September 2009. The sunset is **conditional**: § 318.9 applies only if new legislation establishes applicable breach-notification requirements and implementing regulations take effect; the regulation does not state an automatic 17 February 2026 expiration date.
